Peå Granberg (b.1988, Umeå), furniture designer & artist based in Stockholm.

The basis of his practice is to merge form, function and sculpture and turn it into furniture, products or objects. As unique pieces, limited editions or possibly into some sort of serial production. The process is always founded out of the recycling and upcycling of found materials.
